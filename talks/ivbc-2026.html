Benchmarking Heterogeneous Audio Biomarkers for Respiratory Health Question Answering

International Vocal Biomarkers Conference (IVBC 2026), organised by eVoiceNet COST Action · Luxembourg

Our work on vocal and respiratory audio biomarkers for respiratory health question answering was selected for an oral presentation at the International Vocal Biomarkers Conference (IVBC 2026), held on 29–30 June 2026 in Luxembourg.

The conference was organised by eVoiceNet, the COST Action European Network for Vocal Biomarkers in Healthcare, of which I am a member. It brought together researchers, clinicians, AI specialists and speech scientists working to move voice-based biomarkers from research into responsible, real-world healthcare applications.

eVoiceNet collaborates with the North American Bridge2AI-Voice consortium through the International VOCAL Initiative, working towards shared definitions, greater standardisation and the clinically meaningful and ethical use of voice as a health biomarker.
