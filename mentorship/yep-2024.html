YEP, We Can! — Young Women Empowerment Program

Fondazione Ortygia

Mentor for YEP, We Can!, a programme of the Ortygia Foundation supporting young female students in Southern Italy in defining and pursuing their personal, academic and professional goals.

The programme lasts six months, with six one-hour mentoring meetings held in person or remotely.
