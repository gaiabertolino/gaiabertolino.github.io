AIM: PhD programme

University of Cambridge

Mentor for AIM: PhD, a free widening-participation programme run by the University of Cambridge that supports prospective students in preparing their PhD applications to Cambridge.

The 2026 cycle runs from July 2026 to January 2027 through a structured six-month mentoring arrangement, for applicants planning to start their PhD in 2027-28.
